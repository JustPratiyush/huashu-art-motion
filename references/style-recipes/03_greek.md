# 03_greek · 530 BC Attic black-figure pottery — notes on drawing it in code

File: `scenes/03_greek.js` (pure Canvas 2D, no images). Final selfcheck: warm max 15.9ms / mean 7.6ms / cold 80ms, determinism passes, 0.2↔0.7s motion diff: 22% of pixels moving.

## The trick in one sentence
Black-figure = the trio of **black-glaze silhouette + incised lines + added color**; all three are required:
- Silhouette: every person/object is filled with `#1b120d` (near-black, warm), no outlines; forms read from the silhouette against the clay ground.
- Incised lines: "cut through" the black silhouette with bright clay orange `#e8904c` 1.5–2px fine lines (drapery folds, facial features, boundaries where limbs overlap).
- Added color: purple-red `#8a2a37` bands (hair ribbon, cuffs, hem, collar, cushion) + small white dots (`#f6ead6`, r≈2, spacing 11–17px).
Get these three right and it reads as a Greek vase at a glance, with no texture needed.

## Palette
Clay `#dc6a2f`, light clay (vase panel) `#e8803f`, black glaze `#1b120d`, purple-red `#8a2a37`, white `#f6ead6`, incised line `#e8904c`, blonde `#f2bd48`, clay reserve (cat's white areas) `#f3cfa4`, cat orange `#f2913f`, cat nose `#e7897a`.

## Pipeline and parameters
1. **Clay ground (cached `gr_bg`)**: computed per-pixel at low resolution 480×270: fbm(×0.016, 4 octaves) mottling ±30 + **horizontal banding noise** `noise(x*0.006, y*0.85)` (throwing marks) ±9, upscaled to 1920×1080 (low frequencies are blurry anyway; saves 16× computation); then 560 random fine horizontal lines (length 150–1050, alpha 0.03–0.08, dark/light) as fine wheel marks; 420 small dark dots + 120 light dots; two layers of `P.grain`; 5 cracks (one segment per 9px, ±2px jitter, with a faint 1px-offset light edge drawn along the lower side so the crack immediately has thickness). A warm radial brightening of 0.16 at the center. Cold start ~80ms for the full frame.
2. **Vignette (cached `gr_vig`)**: radial 380→1180, outer edge rgba(30,6,0,.62); the last layer, covering the whole frame (including the bands).
3. **Static black-figure layer (cached `gr_static`)**: picture frame (18px black border + string of white dots at 11.6px spacing + inner fine line), grapevine (sine stem + triangular grape bunches of 3-2-1 dots), table (legs with animal-paw feet), turned chair legs (a width table [y,w] mirrored as a polyline), swan-neck chair back (13px thick stroked curve + oval head + triangular beak), amphora (24-point symmetric outline → `RIG.smooth`; the vase panel is reserved in clay + a small black cat, ray pattern at the base, a vertical gloss gradient at 0.09), two Greek inscriptions.
4. **Greek inscriptions**: the font library has no Greek glyphs → a hand-written **stroke table** (each letter 1–3 polylines in a 0..1 unit cell). Letter height 30, stroke width 3.4, each letter's height ×(0.9–1.06) random, baseline jitter ±6%, vertex jitter ±0.7px; Ο drawn as a small circle (height 0.25 — the original has a small o), Ι width 2px. The hand-painted vase-inscription flavor comes mainly from "uneven letter heights + small o".
5. **Scrolling bands**: the top meander (period 60px, 7px black line, unit polyline `[[4,46],[4,0],[50,0],[50,34],[18,34],[18,14],[36,14],[36,22]]` + continuous baseline) and the bottom lotus-bud chain + ray pattern (period 76px = two 38px units; odd buds filled purple-red, so the real period is 76) are each pre-rendered as strips W+2P wide, and each frame does `drawImage(strip, -(off mod P))`. Offset curves:
   - Top: `off = 22·lt + 60·easeInOut((lt−0.49)/0.35)`, leftward;
   - Bottom: `off = −(18·lt + 76·easeInOut((lt−0.477)/0.367))`, rightward.
   The original only does one easeInOut period jump at frames 169–191; I added an extra 18–22px/s constant drift so the first 0.47s of this segment visibly moves too.
6. **Motifs inside the frame** (drawn every frame, clipped to the frame): 6 dolphins (hand-written Bézier silhouettes: beak, dorsal fin, pectoral fin, forked tail + belly incised line + white eye dot) swim back and forth ±9px along their own heading, angle ±0.12rad, period 0.85s, phase offset 1.3 between them; the ship bobs ±4px and rocks ±0.025rad around (560,420), period 0.9s. White sail + purple-red seams, crescent hull + scorpion-tail stern, purple-red cabin, reclining figure.
7. **Lyre**: swings around the nail (1050,150) by `0.09·sin(2π·lt/0.8+0.5)` (±5°, ±17px at the sound box, clearly visible within 1 second). Tortoiseshell sound box = black circle + incised ring + 5 small incised circles.
8. **Rosettes**: 8 black oval petals + incised petal divisions + purple-red center, rotating `lt·1.6` rad/s, alternating directions.
9. **Orange-and-white cat** (hand-drawn, not RIG's default polygon; the tail uses `RIG.cat`'s tailPts swinging with choreo.tail): the whole cat scaled 0.9 at (548,903) to fit the standard box; orange silhouette + 3.4px black outline; white chest/paws/muzzle in the cream "clay reserve"; **back tabby stripes: 12 points equally spaced by arc length along the back outline, each drawing a tapered stripe toward the body center (575,800)** (length 30–48, root width 10) — far more natural than placing stripes by hand; thigh arc, foreleg vertical stripes; archaic large round eyes (white + black pupil + highlight + a brow arc above), turning into arcs when blinking; purple-red collar with white dots.
10. **Girl**: `RIG.girl({cup,sip,breathe,s:0.97,dy:-14,hair:'long'})` — scaled and lowered so the feet land on the ground line at 903.
    - The body directly fills RIG's Path2Ds (farSleeve/skirt/torso/neck); the far hand is replaced with a small ellipse (RIG's farHand looks like a blade inside the black silhouette).
    - **Head drawn by hand**: a Greek profile (straight nose continuing from the forehead, fine chin) + hairstyle outline written in RIG's rest coordinate system, with a canvas transform reproducing RIG's head motion: `translate(neck)·rotate(G.tilt)·translate(−neck)·T`. The same matrix is computed again with `DOMMatrix`, and `Path2D.addPath(faceSil, M)` is used as the clip region for incised lines.
    - **Blonde hair** (the key to recognizing "the blonde girl"): inside the hair clip draw 11 wavy strands on top of the head (quadratic Béziers + normal-direction 2.6px·sin(7πu) wave), 8 long curls down the back (4.5 sine half-waves + a small spiral at the end), a double-spiral bun, 4 small curls on the forehead, two spiral curls at the temples (a 6px black base line first, then 2.2px gold, so they stay visible outside the silhouette). Gold lines need 2.5px at 1080p to have enough "weight"; at 2px the thumbnail no longer reads as blonde.
    - Incised lines: 16 drapery folds (clipped inside the body), almond eye (light sclera + black pupil; a frontal eye on a profile face), brow, nostril, lips, chin. **Boundary where the near arm overlaps the body/head**: clip(body ∪ head), then stroke incised lines along the arm's outline — black on black can only be separated this way, so the face doesn't smear into one blob when the hand is raised to the mouth.
    - Added color: 15px purple-red band with white dots on the hem, cuff bands (clipped inside the upper arm, a 15px band drawn along the upper arm's normal + incised lines above and below), bracelet, 8 small four-petal white-dot flowers, hair ribbon.
    - Kylix (wide shallow two-handled cup, half-width 32) follows `G.cup` (including tilt), with a purple-red line at the rim; steam is three "≀" fine black lines with phase `t·9`.

## Things that move (all visible within 0–0.94s)
Cup drinking (choreo.cup lifts to the lips in 0.9s + slight head tilt), cat tail/blink/breathing, top and bottom bands scrolling (drift + beat-synced period jump), lyre swinging, ship bobbing, dolphins swimming, rosettes rotating, steam twisting.

## Pitfalls we hit
- Scaling RIG's hair outline directly made the back of the head too wide (in the first version it reached x=1450 and the head looked like a helmet); per the design sheet it was pulled in to 1424, with the long hair only hanging to y≈558.
- With the cup at the lips, black hand, black cup and black face smeared together: ① incised boundary between arm and head ② smaller cup (half-width 44→32) ③ keep a purple-red line at the rim.
- The top of RIG's cat head reaches y=535, while the standard box's cat top is 610; without scaling it presses on the bottom of the frame.
- When the bottom band alternates odd/even buds, the real period is 2 units (76px); the pre-rendered strip must wrap at 76, otherwise the color jumps every 38px.
- `P.grain`'s seed = key length; the keys of the two grain layers must have different lengths, otherwise the patterns are identical.
- **The cup's end pose must fit the era (fix after review)**: RIG's cup→mouth endpoint is near the tip of the nose with a cup tilt of −0.45rad; with the wide, shallow kylix it looked like a beak/pipe stuck into the face, and the hand covered the mouth and chin. Replaced with a custom IK: the lip point rest(1243,391) is transformed to the screen with the head DOMMatrix, cup rim center = lip point −(30,−1) (the right end of the rim just touching the lips), tilt −0.08·cup (≤5°), the hand's IK target = rim +(lerp(32,6,cup), 30), i.e., supporting the cup from beneath its foot; `RIG.ik2(S, hand target, 165s, 122s, −1)` + `RIG.taper` produce the upper arm/forearm. The forearm stands upright in front of the chest, and the profile of nose, lips and chin stays fully visible. The steam follows the rim.

## v1 review fixes (cat redone in black-figure + more top-band scrolling)
- **The cat must use the same technique as the person**: a cartoon cat with big bright-orange round eyes broke the black-figure illusion at a glance. Changed to a black-glaze silhouette, with "orange and white" conveyed only by added color and incised zones: a 26px purple-red saddle band along the back outline, a purple-red patch on top of the head (46×22 ellipse clipped inside the head), tail black with a 7px purple-red core; all tabby stripes are clay-orange `#f2913f` 3px **incised lines** (14 curved lines equally spaced by arc length along the back toward the body center + 3 on the thigh + 3 on the foreleg + a ring every 2 points on the tail); white chest/muzzle/paws in white `#efe1c8`, with black incised toes on the paws; eyes changed to archaic incised almond eyes (white sclera + vertical-oval black pupil + small incised line at the outer corner), a blink is a downward-curving incised line; nose purple-red; whiskers on the left as incised lines (landing on the black face), on the right as black lines (landing on clay).
- **Scrolling a periodic pattern by a whole number of periods = first and last frames identical**: after the top band jumps one 60px period, the image is exactly the same as before the jump, which looks like "not moving" when you compare stills or sampled frames. The constant drift went from 22→48px/s (bottom band 18→30px/s), guaranteeing a phase difference between any two frames.
- Whole-film static-segment motion (480×270 mean frame diff): 03_greek 1.87 → 2.02, original 2.06. warm max 17ms.
