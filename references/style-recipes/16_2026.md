# 16_2026 · 2026 flat vector illustration + the whole film's closing gag

All acting is written in **whole-film frame numbers `F = t*60`**, with the key numbers copied one by one from §4 of `../breakdown/storyboard.md`, so it can be checked against the original frame by frame.
Comparison contact sheets (left original | right recreation): `../renders/16_2026/cmp_E1…E4_*.jpg`, clip `clip_2026.mp4` (10.8–15.06s).

## Style (Dribbble flat + grain)
- Solid color blocks, no outlines; volumes are separated only by "blocks one shade darker in the same color family" (sweater's light diagonal block `#f08b7d`, cat's thigh `#f3ad4c` + one dark arc edge).
- **Grain stippling `stipple()`**: scatter 2px square dots inside a shape, with density as a function of position — pink circle `0.12 + 0.75·clamp((y-cy+120)/420)` (denser toward the bottom), the pale purple block denser toward the lower right, the sun denser toward the lower right. All static parts go into the cache `fl_bg`.
- After the character layer is drawn, a static grain texture is overlaid with `source-atop` (5% dark dots α70, 3.5% light dots α90), so grain lands only on the characters.
- Light cone: 26000 dots, width growing linearly with y 56→150, opacity decaying with y, denser near the center; cached as a 340×420 texture that swings with the ceiling lamp around (1010,0).

## The girl (custom poses on top of RIG)
- Torso/neck anchors: `RIG.girl({s:1.12})`; the head calls `RIG.girl({s:1.344, dy:43.7})` once more — equivalent to scaling the head 1.2× around the neck point, the big-head proportions of flat illustration. Face, bangs, back hair, bun, and eye/lip/ear anchors all come from it.
- Arms: `RIG.ik2(shoulder, target, 150, 140, -1)`, with three sets of target points blended by progress: typing (both hands rising and falling 7px alternately at 22 rad/s on the keyboard) → petting the cat (688–704, the near hand draws small circles on the cat's back) → gasp (791–798, raised in front of the chin with `ease.outBack`, fingers spread).
- Expressions: closed-eye arcs + upturned mouth (<686) → dot eyes + highlights, small o mouth (from 686, the o pops out with outBack) → big eyes + wide-open mouth + tongue (from 791) → after frame 838, a squinting "<" (the original's last frame also has closed eyes).
- Stress lines: 3, radiating from above and in front of the head, length popping out with outBack; color from pale pink at 793 → full red at 796 (RGB interpolation); 830–888 random jitter ±2.5px every 2 frames, the head jittering ±2px in sync.
- Trouser legs/socks/shoes drawn by hand (RIG has a long skirt).

## The cat (pose library + interpolation)
Each pose is a set of local keypoints (facing right, origin = centroid): body 10 points, white chest 6 points, thigh ellipse, 3 back stripes, head, two forelegs, hind leg, 4 Bézier points for the tail, gy (feet to centroid).
Poses: `SIT_F` (sitting on the floor, measured from the original at 10.95s), `SIT_T` (sitting on the table; measured facing left in the original and stored mirrored facing right), `LEAP` (stretched horizontally), `LAND` (front paws pointing down). `mixPose` lerps every value.
Transform: `translate(centroid) → rotate [around the feet or around the centroid] → scale(dir·sx, sy)`; squash and stretch are both around the feet, so the feet stay on the ground when crouching.

## Cup and shattering
- Cup x: linear interpolation of the storyboard's 16 measured points (744:981 … 796:801); from 790–796 it starts tipping outward -0.12.
- Fall 796–815: Catmull-Rom through 9 measured center points (measured gravity ≈1.75px/frame²), rotation `-0.12 - 2.3·q^1.3`; 6 coffee drops behind it take the cup's positions at past intervals of 1.3 frames, smaller the earlier they are.
- Impact at 815: 7 impact rays fading out over 8 frames; two large shards on Lagrange parabolas through the storyboard's three points (818/824/834), stopping when they land at 834; 9 small fragments on parabolas, each stopping at its own landing height 893–911; coffee stain width 818:140 → 822:220 → 826:235 (ease.out); two gray ripples expanding to 560/460 wide, fading out before 838; the half-ring handle + tea-bag tag stay on the floor.
- Pat motion lines: 3 short lines to the right of the cup, expanding outward and fading over 12 frames after each hit.

## Secondary motion in the environment
- Ceiling lamp: `damped(F, 686, 0.055rad, period 26 frames, decay 22 frames) + damped(F, 830, 0.045, 22, 20)`; the light cone swings with it.
- Plants: `damped(F, 697, 0.035, 22, 18)` (sway) + `damped(F, 821, 0.05, 9, 26)` (shake, short period); each monstera leaf also has a little self-sway with a different phase.
- Clouds move left at 15px/s (0.25px/frame); a small plane at 177px/s trailing a dashed contrail (at x≈440 at 12.0s and x≈750 at 13.75s, matching the original); sun rays rotating at 0.35 rad/s; 17 decorative symbols boiling at 4fps (±1.5px, ±0.09rad).
